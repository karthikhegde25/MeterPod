MeterPod.
Twenty tools. One pocket.

A protractor, a level, a compass, a speedometer, a QR generator, and sixteen more — built from the sensors already in your phone. No ads, no accounts, no in‑app purchases.

Motion & orientation

Know exactly where you stand.

Seven tools built on the accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, and GPS already inside your phone.

Angle & Protractor

Rest an edge on any surface and read the acute angle live, or drag a full-size on-screen protractor to measure a drawing or photo directly against your glass.

  • Live slope (rise/run) alongside the angle
  • Large draggable dial with lock and fine-step controls

Digital Level & Compass

A bubble level with separate X/Y tilt readouts for getting shelves and frames perfectly true, plus a magnetic compass for heading.

Speed, Odometer & Pedometer

GPS speedometer with a live gauge, a distance tracker for a walk or drive, and a step counter — for whenever you need to know how fast, how far, or how many steps.

Sensing & detection

See what the room won't tell you.

Four tools that turn hidden fields, sound, and light into a number you can act on.

Metal Detector & Light Detector

The magnetometer flags nearby metal with a field-strength reading and a rough direction arrow. The ambient light sensor reads lux on a log scale, from moonlight to direct sun.

Sound Level

A microphone-based dB meter with dominant frequency and a live waveform — check a room's noise level or tune by ear.

Sensor Tester

Every raw sensor your device exposes, listed and live — for checking a sensor actually works, or just seeing what's inside.

Camera & codes

Look closer. Generate anything.

Magnifier

Your camera as a magnifying glass, with zoom and flashlight — for small print, tiny screws, and splinters.

QR Generator

Nine content types — text, URL, Wi‑Fi, contact card, email, SMS, phone, location, and calendar event — with custom colors, dot shapes, and an optional center logo.

  • Wi‑Fi QR codes your guests can scan to join, no typing a password
  • Save to gallery or share straight from the app

Everyday tools

The small stuff, sorted.

Ruler, Calculator & Unit Converter

A screen ruler calibrated to your actual display, a scientific calculator with live-preview evaluation, and a unit converter for the conversions you always forget.

Coin Toss & Word Counter

A real animated coin flip for settling an argument fast, and a word & character counter for when a form has a limit and you're right up against it.

Diagnostics

Everything about the device you're holding.

Screen Info

Resolution, density, refresh rate, and every other display detail your phone reports, in one readable list.

Network & Wi‑Fi Info

SSID, signal strength, IP configuration, DNS, DHCP lease, and every Wi‑Fi capability your hardware supports — everything Android will hand over about the network you're on.
